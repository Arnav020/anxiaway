From signal to next step

What happens to a reading.

  1. WearableECG, pulse, skin conductance and movement, sampled on the device.
  2. Signal LayerFixed code compares you with your own baseline, and rejects movement and weak signals.
  3. Anxiety modelDesigned for five inputs. Whichever are missing are marked absent, so it still gives a reading.
  4. Care agentUses the reading beside your answers to plan the next step. A high reading only makes it more careful.
Why EEG isn't on the device

Five inputs in the model, four on your body.

Our model design learns from brain activity (EEG) in clinical-grade research recordings. Low-cost EEG sensors are too noisy to match that, so we left EEG off the wearable instead of feeding the model a poor signal. The model is being trained to work when inputs are missing, and here EEG is always marked absent.

The model is still being trained and validated. Until it's connected, any model reading in this app is a labelled simulation.

What a reading can't tell you

A raised heart rate isn't anxiety.

Caffeine, exercise, illness, heat and talking all raise it. That's why Toward compares you with your own baseline, keeps the reading apart from your answers, and never calls a reading a diagnosis.

For the team: how the device connects

This app pulls the latest readings from the same API our mobile app uses. Set TOWARD_DEVICE_API_URL on the server (and TOWARD_DEVICE_API_KEY if needed), and the wearable option switches on everywhere. One sample a second is expected.
