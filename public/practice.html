    How practice is kept safe
    A recent check comes first

    Guided sessions need a check from the last 24 hours. A severe score, a heart condition or a crisis answer means no session, and a route to real support.

    The level never jumps on stress

    Rising arousal doesn't make it easier or harder. Only a calm reading lets the next step move up, and a safety limit pauses everything.

    Stop always works

    One tap, or say stop. It ends at once with no persuasion. Crisis language gets fixed words written by people, never a model.
