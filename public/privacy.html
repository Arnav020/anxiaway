Privacy and terms

Toward is a student research prototype for anxiety practice, from Thapar Institute. The panelists are AI generated characters. This is a plain description of how it behaves, not formal legal text.

What happens to what you say

What you type or say during a session is sent to Groq so it can transcribe your speech and write the panel's lines. Groq's own terms apply to that processing. Please don't share anything you wouldn't want processed by a third party.

Your questionnaire scores and body signal data aren't sent to language models. The safety rules read them on our server.

What we keep

We have no accounts and no database. Your session travels in a signed token held by your browser, and it's gone when you close the page. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as addresses and request times.

Your microphone

The microphone is only used if you choose voice, and only while you're in the room. Audio is sent for transcription once each answer ends.

Terms of use

This prototype isn't therapy, diagnosis or medical advice, and nothing has shown that it helps. It's intended for adults. Use it at your own discretion, and stop whenever you want.

If you need support now

If you may be in danger, call your local emergency number. In India, Tele-MANAS is 14416. In the UK and Ireland, Samaritans are 116 123.
