Privacy and terms

AnxiAway is a student research prototype for anxiety screening and practice support, from Thapar Institute. The guides are AI generated characters, not clinicians. This is a plain description of how it behaves, not formal legal text.

What happens to what you say

What you type or say, plus your GAD-7 range and strongest concerns, is sent to Groq so it can transcribe your speech and write your plan, your guides' lines and your summary. Groq's own terms apply to that processing. On computers, your browser may also download an open voice model once (about 90 to 330 MB) to speak the guides' lines on your device. Please don't share anything you wouldn't want processed by a third party.

Your individual GAD-7 answers, your safety answers and raw body signal data aren't sent to language models. The safety rules read them on our server. The voices may be generated by Groq, or in your browser using the Kokoro voice model.

Your anxiety checks

Your GAD-7 answers and safety answers are scored on our server by fixed code, and aren't stored there or sent to any language model. Each check, with its score and any body reading, is saved only in this browser. To plan your next step, the agent receives your scores, dates and reading labels, and never your individual answers.

Body readings

Today the readings are a synthetic demo stream, and aren't your data. Once our wearable is linked, its readings will pass through our server to be read by fixed code, and won't be stored there. No language model receives a raw reading.

What we keep

We have no accounts and no database. Your session travels in a signed token held by your browser, and it's gone when you close the page. Your checks and your track record are saved only in this browser's own storage, on this device. When the agent reviews it, the record passes through our server to Groq, and our server keeps no copy. You can delete everything at any time from the Record screen, and clearing your browser data also removes it. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as addresses and request times.

Your microphone

The microphone is only used if you choose voice, and only while you're in the room. Audio is sent for transcription once each answer ends.

Terms of use

This prototype isn't therapy, diagnosis or medical advice, and nothing has shown that it helps. It's intended for adults. Use it at your own discretion, and stop whenever you want.

If you need support now

If you may be in danger, call your local emergency number. In India, Tele-MANAS is 14416. In the UK and Ireland, Samaritans are 116 123.
