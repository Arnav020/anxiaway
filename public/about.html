Agentic AI Hackathon 2026 · Theme 6

Agentic AI for Healthcare & Human Well-being.

The brief asks for healthcare monitoring, early detection, accessibility, rehabilitation or personalised well-being. Toward answers each of them for one condition, anxiety, with an agent that acts.

Healthcare monitoring

The Vitals screen reads ECG, pulse, skin conductance and movement in 10 second windows, against a personal baseline. Repeated GAD-7 checks are tracked over time.

Early detection

Screening at the door, a daily life question, and a trend rule: scores that rise across three checks into the moderate range, or a big effect on daily life, send the person toward a professional early.

Accessibility

Voice or text, at any hour, in a browser, with no account and no hardware needed to start. Works without a language model if the network fails.

Rehabilitation

A graded ladder back into avoided situations, following the structure of exposure based approaches: guided rehearsal first, then small real steps.

Personalised well-being

Your screening, your baseline, your own predictions and outcomes. The plan is built from your record, and nobody else's averages.

And truly agentic

The agent chooses which tools to call to read your record, decides among the steps safety rules allow, schedules a follow up, and replans from the outcome. Your record persists between visits, and it reviews it each time you return.

The agent loop

Sense, decide, act, follow up, replan.

  1. 1

    Sense

    A check gives three sources: answers, a body reading and the model's verdict. Fixed code scores them and keeps them apart.

  2. 2

    Decide

    The agent calls tools to read the screening trend and the prediction record. Fixed code sets which steps are allowed, and the agent chooses among them: check, test, log, or a person.

  3. 3

    Act

    It writes a graded plan, runs a guided session with three role-playing agents, and sets one real world step.

  4. 4

    Follow up

    It schedules when to ask what actually happened, and the person records the outcome and how they coped.

  5. 5

    Replan

    The outcome changes the next step. A hard result or a rising trend routes to a counsellor instead of another test.

Under the hood

Language models propose. Fixed code decides.

In healthcare, you can't hand everything to a model. Here's what happens at each step of a guided session, usually in about a second.

Checks every response before a model sees it. Handles crisis language, physical symptoms, safety limits and session limits. It can overrule every agent, and it isn't a language model, so it can't be talked out of it.

A clinical design choice

Don't rescue by default.

Backing off every time anxiety rises risks teaching one lesson: escape is how you get through it. Toward holds the level, and pauses only at a safety limit, or the moment you say stop.

Anxiety-like arousal rises, and the challenge holds. Stopping stays one tap away the whole time.
