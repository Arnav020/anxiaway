What you reportGAD-7, a screening questionnaire used widely in clinics.

What the model saysOur anxiety model's reading of those signals, once it's connected.
  • It screens. It doesn't diagnose.

    This is a student research prototype. It can't tell you whether you have an anxiety disorder, and nobody has shown yet that it helps.

  • Your answers stay on this device.

    Our server scores them with fixed code and keeps nothing. No language model sees your individual answers or your safety answers. Privacy note.

  • Safety questions come first.

    If this isn't the right tool for you today, we'll say so and point you to better support.

Step 1 of 4 · Safety

Three safety questions first.

Please answer honestly. A yes doesn't mean anything is wrong with you. It means a person can help more than this tool can today.

Have you had thoughts of harming yourself recently?
Are you in crisis, or feeling unsafe, right now?
Do you have a heart condition, or has a doctor told you to avoid stress?

Step 2 of 4 · What you report

Over the last two weeks, how often have you been bothered by these?

This is the GAD-7, a screening questionnaire. There are no right answers. Go with your first instinct.

    Step 3 of 4 · Daily life

    How difficult have these made your daily life?

    Your work or studies, looking after things at home, or getting along with other people. A low score with a big effect on daily life still matters, so we ask.
