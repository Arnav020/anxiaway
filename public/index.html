Agentic AI for Healthcare & Human Well-being.

Toward answers each part of the brief, for one specific problem: anxiety around high stakes speaking, like vivas, interviews and presentations.

Healthcare monitoring

A four sensor wearable design reads ECG, PPG, skin conductance and motion. This demo replays a synthetic recording of those channels.

Early detection

It spots rising arousal against your own baseline, as an anxiety-like signal, while rejecting movement and bad readings. It doesn't diagnose.

Accessibility

Voice or text, at any hour, in a browser. Practice is available when counselling isn't, and a referral is offered when it's needed.

Rehabilitation

A graded ladder of practice, built back from your real event, follows the step by step structure of exposure based approaches.

Personalised well-being

Your baseline, your feared prediction, your ladder and your own real world mission. Nothing is one size fits all.

And truly agentic

It sets a goal, plans over days, chooses tactics, acts in the room and replans from evidence, inside limits it can't override.

The anxiety problem

Anxiety grows when we avoid. Facing it is hard to arrange.

Anxiety disorders are the most common mental disorders in the world. Avoiding a feared situation keeps anxiety going, and facing it step by step is a core treatment approach. But nobody can summon an interview panel at midnight before a viva.

From our capstone research

Built on our multimodal anxiety project.

Toward grows out of our final year capstone at Thapar Institute: an AI driven multimodal system for detecting an anxiety-like state. The hackathon adds the agent that acts on it.

  • Working prototype. ECG, PPG, skin conductance and motion on an ESP32, streaming to a mobile app.
  • Multimodal design. A per sensor encoder and cross attention fusion design, with availability masks so a lost sensor lowers confidence instead of breaking things.
  • Clinical grounding. GAD-7 screening, and careful language: an anxiety-like state, never a diagnosis.
  • Honest status. Our detection model is still being validated, so this demo uses transparent rules on synthetic data, not the model's output.
How it works

An anxious thought becomes an experiment.

  1. 1

    Say what you predict

    "If I pause to think, the panel will decide I don't know my subject." You rate how likely that feels.

  2. 2

    The Planner makes it testable

    It rewrites the fear as something observable, and builds a ladder of steps back from your real deadline.

  3. 3

    Face a live panel

    Three AI generated panelists question you by voice. Whisper transcribes your answers and your pause is timed from the mic.

  4. 4

    The room adapts

    Anxiety-like arousal from the wearable steers the challenge, without ever telling you that you look anxious. Fixed safety rules stay in charge.

  5. 5

    Check the prediction

    You rate it again, and take one small mission into real life, where the actual evidence lives.

The rule that sets it apart

Don't rescue by default.

Adaptive systems often back off as soon as stress rises. We think that risks teaching one lesson: escape is how you get through it. Toward holds the level, and eases only at a safety limit, or the moment you say stop.

Arousal rises, and the challenge holds. Stopping stays one tap away the whole time.

Under the hood

Language models propose. Fixed code decides.

Most agent demos hand everything to the model. In a safety sensitive setting, that's the wrong design. Here's what happens every time you answer, usually in about a second.

Turns ECG, PPG, skin conductance and motion into a single state label. It calibrates on neutral speech, rejects movement and falls back to PPG if ECG drops. No language model ever reads a raw vital.

Sessions are also stateless: everything travels in a signed token, so safety counters can't be edited in the browser.

Safety

Built to stop, not to persuade.

Screened at the door

GAD-7 at 15 or above, a heart condition or a crisis means no exposure session, and a referral instead.

Stop always works

One tap, or say stop. It ends immediately with no persuasion, and even rate limits can't block it.

Crisis means fixed words

Self harm language ends the scene with support details written by people. That text is never generated, and never reaches a model.

Limits pause the room

At the safety limit the panel leans back, the lights lift and you choose what happens next. A second limit ends the session.

Rules and thresholds are starting design values. They haven't been clinically validated and still need review by a counsellor.

Questions

What people ask.

Is this therapy?

No. It's a research prototype for practising feared speaking situations. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. If you're struggling, please speak to a counsellor or doctor.

Can it detect anxiety?

Not as a diagnosis. It tracks change against your own neutral baseline and treats that as an anxiety-like signal. Arousal and anxiety aren't the same, and research shows they often disagree. Validating a detection model is the work of our capstone.

Is the body data in this demo real?

No, it's a synthetic recording, labelled on screen, so judges can see how the logic behaves and even inject events. Your voice, your pause timing and the agents are live.

Are the panelists real people?

No. They're AI generated characters, and their questions are written live by language models.

How is this related to your capstone?

It shares the same anxiety focus, 4 sensor prototype, multimodal design and GAD-7 grounding. The capstone asks whether we can detect an anxiety-like state. Toward asks what an agent should do next.

Do I need the wearable?

Not for this demo. The design treats sensors as optional, so missing channels lower confidence rather than breaking the session.

Isn't provoking anxiety dangerous?

It can be, which is why there's screening, consent, a stop control and limits in fixed code. It's aimed at mild to moderate performance anxiety. These rules still need professional review.

What happens to what I say?

Your text and audio go to Groq to generate the session. We have no database and no accounts. Read the privacy note for details.
