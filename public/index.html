Agentic AI for Healthcare & Human Well-being.

Toward answers each part of the brief for one condition that affects hundreds of millions of people: anxiety.

Healthcare monitoring

Our wearable design reads ECG, PPG, skin conductance and motion, and our model turns them into an anxiety-like verdict. The demo uses a labelled simulation until that's integrated.

Early detection

GAD-7 screening at the door, then rising arousal spotted against your own baseline, while movement and bad readings are rejected. It flags, and never diagnoses.

Accessibility

Voice or text, at any hour, in a browser. Support between appointments, and a clear referral whenever a person is the better answer.

Rehabilitation

A graded ladder helps you step back into what anxiety made you avoid, following the structure of exposure based approaches.

Personalised well-being

Your screening, your baseline, your feared prediction and your practice plan. You leave with a summary to keep or share with a counsellor.

And truly agentic

It assesses, builds a graded plan, chooses tactics, acts, and updates your next step from what happened, inside safety limits it can't override.

The anxiety problem

Only about 1 in 4 people with anxiety receive treatment.

Anxiety disorders are the most common mental disorders in the world. Avoidance keeps anxiety going, and step by step practice is a core part of treatment, yet it's hard to access between appointments, or without one.

How it works

From a check-in to a care plan you can act on.

  1. 1

    Check in

    GAD-7 screening, your situation, and what your anxiety predicts. You see your result straight away.

  2. 2

    Connect readings

    Your wearable and our anxiety model supply a live reading and verdict. Integrating soon, with a labelled demo stream today.

  3. 3

    The agent decides

    It picks a care pathway from your screening, and writes a graded plan toward what you've been avoiding.

  4. 4

    Guided session

    AI guides role play your situation by voice. Readings and the verdict steer each step, and safety rules stay in charge.

  5. 5

    Reflect and share

    A few questions afterwards, then a health summary with your GAD-7, readings, next steps and a real world test.

From our capstone research

Built on our multimodal anxiety project.

Toward grows out of our final year capstone at Thapar Institute: an AI driven multimodal system for detecting an anxiety-like state. The hackathon adds the agent that acts on it.

  • Working prototype. ECG, PPG, skin conductance and motion on an ESP32, streaming to a mobile app.
  • Multimodal design. A per sensor encoder and cross attention fusion design, with availability masks so a lost sensor lowers confidence instead of breaking things.
  • Clinical grounding. GAD-7 screening, and careful language: an anxiety-like state, never a diagnosis.
  • Honest status. Our detection model is still being trained and validated, so this demo uses transparent rules on synthetic data, not the model's output.
A clinical design choice

Don't rescue by default.

Backing off every time anxiety rises risks teaching one lesson: escape is how you get through it. Toward holds the level, and pauses only at a safety limit, or the moment you say stop.

Anxiety-like arousal rises, and the challenge holds. Stopping stays one tap away the whole time.

Under the hood

Language models propose. Fixed code decides.

In healthcare, you can't hand everything to a model. Here's what happens at each step, usually in about a second.

Checks every response before a model sees it. Handles crisis language, physical symptoms, safety limits and session limits. It can overrule every agent, and it isn't a language model, so it can't be talked out of it.

Safety

Built to stop, not to persuade.

Screened at the door

GAD-7 at 15 or above, a heart condition or a crisis means no session, and a referral to real support instead.

Stop always works

One tap, or say stop. It ends immediately with no persuasion, and even rate limits can't block it.

Crisis means fixed words

Self harm language ends the session with support details written by people. That text is never generated, and never reaches a model.

Limits pause everything

At the safety limit the session pauses, you can breathe for a while, and you choose what happens next. A second limit ends it.

Rules and thresholds are starting design values. They haven't been clinically validated and still need review by a counsellor.

Questions

What people ask.

Is this therapy?

No. It's a research prototype for anxiety screening and practice support. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. If you're struggling, please speak to a counsellor or doctor.

Can it detect anxiety?

Not as a diagnosis. It tracks change against your own baseline and treats that as an anxiety-like signal. Readings and feelings often disagree. Validating a detection model is the work of our capstone.

Is the wearable data real?

In this demo, no. It's a synthetic recording and a simulated verdict, both labelled on screen, while the app and model are being integrated. Your answers, your voice and the agents are live.

Are the guides real people?

No. They're AI generated characters, not clinicians, and their words are written live by language models.

How does this relate to your capstone?

It shares the same anxiety focus, 4 sensor prototype, multimodal design and GAD-7 grounding. The capstone asks whether we can detect an anxiety-like state. Toward asks what a care agent should do next.

Isn't facing anxiety risky?

It can be, which is why there's screening, consent, a stop control and limits in fixed code. It's aimed at mild to moderate anxiety. These rules still need professional review.

What happens to what I share?

Your words and screening band go to Groq to write your plan and summary. We have no database and no accounts. Read the privacy note.
