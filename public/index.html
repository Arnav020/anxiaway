How the agent works
The anxiety problem

Only about 1 in 4 people with anxiety receive treatment.

Anxiety disorders are the most common mental disorders in the world. Avoidance keeps anxiety going, and step by step practice is a core part of treatment, yet it's hard to access between appointments, or without one.

470Mpeople had an anxiety disorder in 2023WHO fact sheet 27.6%of people with one received any treatment, across 21 countriesAlonso et al., 2018 46.8%of anxiety treatments used exposure, in a survey of 684 German therapistsPittig et al., 2019

The wearable

Built on our multimodal anxiety project.

AnxiAway grows out of our final year capstone at Thapar Institute: an AI driven multimodal system for detecting an anxiety-like state. This app is where its readings become action.

  • Working prototype. ECG, PPG, skin conductance and motion on an ESP32, streaming to a mobile app.
  • Five inputs in the model, four on the body. Low-cost EEG is too noisy to match research-grade recordings, so EEG stays off the device and is marked absent at run time.
  • Ready to connect. The app already takes readings from the device's API. One setting switches it on.
  • Honest status. The model is still being trained and validated, so every reading in this demo is synthetic and labelled.
Safety

Built to stop, not to persuade.

These rules are fixed code. No language model writes them, runs them or can argue with them.

  1. Ifyou answer yes to a safety questionThenyou get real support details at once, written by people, before any screening
  2. Ifyour score is severe, or you have a heart conditionThenno guided session starts, and a counsellor or doctor comes first
  3. Ifyou tap stop, or say stopThenit ends immediately, with no persuasion, and nothing can block it
  4. Ifyour reading stays far above your baselineThenthe session pauses and you choose what happens next. A second time ends it
  5. Ifyour scores rise across three checks into the moderate rangeThenthe agent's next step becomes a person, not another exercise
  6. Ifa language model writes anythingThenit never saw a raw vital or an individual answer, and its words are checked before you see them

Rules and thresholds are starting design values. They haven't been clinically validated and still need review by a counsellor.

Questions

What people ask.

Is this therapy, or a diagnosis?

No. It's a research prototype for anxiety screening and practice support. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. If you're struggling, please speak to a counsellor or doctor.

Can it detect anxiety?

Not as a diagnosis. GAD-7 is a screening questionnaire, and the wearable tracks change against your own baseline as an anxiety-like signal. The two often disagree, which is why we show them separately. Validating a detection model is the work of our capstone.

Is the wearable data real?

In this demo, no. It's a synthetic stream and a simulated model reading, both labelled on screen, while the device and model are being integrated. Your answers, your voice and the agents are live.

How is this different from a wellness chatbot?

You don't chat with it. It measures, plans, schedules a follow up and changes its plan from what really happened. Language models write words, while fixed code makes every safety and scoring decision.

What if my worry comes true?

Then that goes in your record too, along with how bad it was and how you coped. AnxiAway never tells you that your anxiety is wrong. If coping has been hard, it suggests talking to a counsellor or someone you trust.

Do I need an account?

No. Your checks and your record are saved in your own browser, on your device, and we keep no copy. That means they won't follow you to another device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Are the guides real people?

No. They're AI generated characters, not clinicians, and their words are written live by language models.

Isn't facing anxiety risky?

It can be, which is why there's screening, consent, a stop control and limits in fixed code. It's aimed at mild to moderate anxiety. These rules still need professional review.

What happens to what I share?

Your words and screening band go to Groq to write your plan and summary, and your record goes there when the agent reviews it. Individual answers and safety answers don't. We have no database and no accounts. Read the privacy note.
